## It isn't the shampoo. That's the first thing to clear up

Olaplex numbers everything, which is where most of the confusion comes from. "No.4" is the Bond Maintenance Shampoo. "No.8" is the Bond Intense Moisture Mask you buy and keep at home. The 4-in-1 is a separate thing entirely: the Nº.4-in-1 Moisture Mask, sold as a professional, in-salon moisture treatment.

If you walked out of a salon with soft, heavy hair and asked what they used, this is probably it. If you were trying to find the shampoo you already own, you're in the wrong listing.

## What's actually in it

The formula is built around moisture rather than pure bond repair. Olaplex's own description lists ceramides 3 and 6, eleven amino acids, panthenol as a humectant, and Bis-Aminopropyl Diglycol Dimaleate — the bond-building ingredient the brand is known for. Squala

A few specifics worth knowing before you buy:

- **pH 4.5**, deliberately acidic. The point is to close the cuticle after a chemical service, which Olaplex says helps with porosity, colour retention, shine, and static.
- **No proteins.** This isn't a protein filler, so it won't behave like a keratin mask.
- **No extra rinse-out conditioner needed.** The mask is meant to close and seal the cuticle itself.
- **One format: 370 ml / 12.55 fl oz.** There is no travel size, no 100 ml, no refill pouch. The pump bottle is the SKU.

Olaplex publishes results from testing on bleached and damaged hair: 74% more moisture, 84% more shine, 84% more smoothness, and 94% of testers seeing more body. Those are the manufacturer's own figures from its own testing, so treat them as what they are — brand testing, not independent lab work.

## How it's used, and the 48-hour rule

In the salon it's applied at the shampoo bowl and rinsed after ten minutes, which is why clients see an immediate difference.

At home, if you have the bottle:

1. Apply 1–3 pumps to clean, wet hair.
2. Comb through so it's evenly distributed.
3. Leave it for 10 minutes.
4. Rinse thoroughly.

No conditioner afterwards. If you're conditioning multiple times between chemical services, Olaplex recommends using the 4-in-1 as the step right before styling, since it also adds body once the hair is dried.

The one restriction that matters: wait **48 hours after a chemical service** before using it. That's Olaplex's own pro guidance, and it applies whether you're doing the treatment at the salon or at home.
